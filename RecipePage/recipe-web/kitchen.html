How to plate as a pro

Make a simple dinner feel special with a little space, colour and texture.

  1. Choose a plate that gives your food room.
  2. Place the main ingredient slightly off-centre.
  3. Add vegetables in small groups and keep the rim clean.
  4. Finish with herbs or a small spoonful of sauce.

In the kitchen / 02

Recipes are for everyone

A little preparation makes cooking much easier.

  1. Read the whole recipe before you start.
  2. Measure ingredients and prepare your tools.
  3. Chop the vegetables before heating the pan.
  4. Taste as you go and adjust the seasoning.

In the kitchen / 03

Meats – how to baste

Spoon warm butter and pan juices over the surface to add flavour.

  1. Brown the meat in a hot pan, then lower the heat.
  2. Add butter, garlic and a sprig of thyme.
  3. Carefully tilt the pan and spoon the melted butter over the meat.
  4. Cook the meat through using an appropriate recipe, then let it rest before slicing.
